## Locked rules (SP6, unchanged)
- Native FDG CT masks of the six structures (liver, spleen, right and left kidney, right lung = lobes 12 + 13 + 14, left lung = lobes 10 + 11; TotalSegmentator total task, `FDG/totseg_24.nii.gz`).
- Each organ or lobe is included if **present** (any Audit 1B category other than absent) and **not fragmented** (frac_largest_component ≥ 0.95) on the **FDG CT**; truncated structures are included; independent of measured displacement.
- An eligible FDG component (26-connectivity, rule A) with **any voxel** in such a mask is V_I (ST2).
- Precedence: V_S → V_I (registration QC, attributed first) → V_I (ST2 organ) → V_I (boundary band) → V_I (coverage) → V_M / V_U. Denominator unchanged.

## Author decisions (12 Nov 2026, 06:54)
1. **D1, any voxel across grids.** A component has a voxel in a mask if the physical centre of **any of its native FDG PET voxels** lies in a voxel of the FDG CT mask: nearest CT voxel, continuous index rounded by floor(x + 0.5) on each axis; a centre outside the CT grid is not in any mask. No supersampling and no partial-volume overlap. (Equivalent to nearest-neighbour resampling of the mask onto the FDG PET grid.)
2. **D2, availability.** From the FDG rows of Audit 1B (`Audit1B_label_level_20261029_134401.csv`, MD5 896a1681e90da4fe793a3a449d737fde): available if the category does not start with `ABSENT` (and `present` = 1; any disagreement between the two stops the run) and frac_largest_component ≥ 0.95. **Lungs: each lobe is assessed separately; the lung mask is the union of the qualifying lobes only.** For each patient and lung, the included and excluded lobes (with the reason) are recorded and a lung missing any lobe is flagged **partial**, so that a partial lung mask is not mistaken for a complete lung. ST2 availability is recorded separately from displacement eligibility (O1). Check (stop on failure): every structure eligible for displacement in O1 (`O1_organ_displacement_20261111_232613.csv`) must be ST2-available with all its labels.
3. **D3, implementation.** Python stage **ST2a** (native-FDG geometry only; no registration, no PSMA data) writes per-component intersection counts and the ST2 flag with MD5s and a manifest; **MATLAB P3 v1.9** inserts the ST2 step in the precedence. **P2 is not rerun** (its flags are unchanged). Gate: with ST2 off, P3 v1.9 must reproduce the primary tables before any ST2 result is written.
4. **D4, reporting.** Each component's volume counts **once** in V_I (ST2). Descriptive organ category = the structure containing the **largest number** of the component's voxel centres; tie → the structure with the **lowest TotalSegmentator label ID** (spleen 1, right kidney 2, left kidney 3, liver 5, left lung 10, right lung 12). The number of components touching more than one structure is reported.
5. **Safeguard.** Registration-first precedence is preserved: **ST2 never reclassifies a component already V_I (registration)**; its volume stays under registration. ST2 adds V_I only for components that would otherwise reach the band, coverage or matching steps.
6. **D5, workflow.** ST2a self-test (synthetic grids, centre-in-mask rule, CT FOV edge, flipped axes, lobe union with an excluded lobe, availability categories, tie rule) → two-patient preflight → full cohort → P3 v1.9 self-test → gate → ST2 run → reconciliation → completion note.

## Interpretation (SP6, SP7)
ST2 estimates the consequence of withholding correspondence decisions in visceral organs. It is conservative only for components intersecting available masks; it is exploratory and does not replace the primary result.
