## Corrections
1. **The reduction in DMTVF_ref under ST2 is mathematically inevitable** and cannot be interpreted as evidence of robustness. Under ST2, components can only move from V_M or V_U to V_I, and the denominator is fixed; DMTVF_ref can therefore only stay the same or fall. The §4 sentence "The low point estimate is therefore not driven by visceral correspondence decisions" is **withdrawn**.
2. **The informative result is the increased uncertainty.** ST2 reclassifies 3,771.0 mL (15.8 % of the cohort's total FDG burden) as indeterminate, including 3,767.9 mL that the primary analysis classified as matched (and 3.1 mL classified as unmatched). This amount of matched burden depends on correspondence decisions in components that contact deformable visceral organs.
3. **The median upper bound of the uncertainty interval increases from 0.27 % to 1.75 %** (IQR 0–2.09 % → 0–9.58 %). This quantifies the effect of conservatively withholding organ-contacting components; it is **not** an estimate of the amount of true discordance.
4. **Unverified claim.** The §4 statement that the withheld volume includes large components that only touch an organ, and is therefore "much larger than intra-organ disease", is **unverified**. It is addressed by a descriptive organ-overlap audit (author approval, 07:31), documented separately.
